## 4. Caveats

- The template numbers from the resume draft (95% navigation success, 85% re-ID recovery, <0.2 m distance error) are **not** the targets of this evaluation and are **not** reproduced here; only measured values are reported.
- DWA/A* are simplified stand-ins for Nav2's planner/controller and the recovery behaviour is a simplified Nav2 behaviour tree.
- The offline detector is HSV colour segmentation on a synthetic renderer; the YOLOv8 adapter exists but is excluded from the automated numbers.
